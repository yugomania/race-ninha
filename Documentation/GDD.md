# Animal Combat Racing — Game Design Document (GDD)

## 1. Vision & Overview
* **Genre**: 3D Arcade Combat Racer
* **Tone**: Vibrant, energetic, colorful, family-friendly
* **Platforms**: Mobile (Android, iOS)
* **Engine**: Unity (Universal Render Pipeline)

## 2. Core Game Loop
1. **Select**: Choose Animal Driver (`AnimalDataSO`) + Formula Kart (`VehicleDataSO`).
2. **Race**: Compete in 4-racer Grand Prix circuits. Use responsive mobile touch steering, drifting, and nitro.
3. **Combat**: Collect floating mystery power-up orbs to trigger tactical cartoon abilities (Energy Orbs, Rolling Bombs, Sonic Roar, Shields).
4. **Finish & Win**: Cross the finish line across 3 laps, trigger podium celebrations, and unlock new circuits and chassis.

## 3. Characters & Roster
* `ANIMAL_Lion_01` ("King Leo"): Confident, charismatic apex racer with the "Apex Sonic Roar" special ability.
* Independent vehicle coupling: Any unlocked animal can drive any unlocked vehicle.

## 4. Visual & Audio Direction
* Stylized 3D cartoon aesthetics inspired by *Beach Buggy Racing 2* and *Mario Kart Tour*.
* High-contrast asphalt with rubber tire marks, 3D raised curbs, living stadium grandstands with clapping spectator crowds.
* Punchy cartoon audio FX: engine revs, tire screeches, comic explosion clatter, and crowd cheering.
