# Animal Combat Racing — Design Pillars

Every system, feature, mechanic, and art asset in *Animal Combat Racing* must be tested and justified against these four pillars. If an idea conflicts with these pillars, it is revised or rejected.

---

## 1. Readable Chaos
* **Definition**: Even with 8 karts on screen, nitro boost flares, and fictional combat weapons discharging simultaneously, the player must always instantly understand:
  1. What just happened.
  2. Who or what hit them.
  3. What tactical action they should take next.
* **Application**: High-contrast weapon silhouettes, distinct audio cues per weapon type, clear screen-edge threat indicators, and non-obscuring visual impact VFX.

---

## 2. Always a Comeback
* **Definition**: Being at the back of the pack never feels hopeless or unrecoverable.
* **Application**: 
  * Rubber-banding dynamically compresses the distance between pack leaders and trailers.
  * Slipstream / drafting gives trailing cars an acceleration slingshot.
  * Weapon pickup probability curves heavily favor trailing racers (giving them Nitro and Homing missiles) while giving leaders defensive shields or hazard drops.
  * A skilled player in 8th place on Lap 2 can still podium on Lap 3.

---

## 3. One-Thumb Mastery
* **Definition**: Controls are simple enough for casual single-touch / two-thumb play, yet possess the nuance and depth required for competitive mastery.
* **Application**:
  * Optional touch-assists from Day 1: Auto-acceleration (no need to hold gas), soft steering assist, and drift assistance.
  * High-skill ceiling: Manual drift-spark timing boosts, precision weapon deflection, and jump-landing boost tricks.

---

## 4. Personality Over Realism
* **Definition**: Character expression, visual charm, and exaggerated cartoon kinetics always take priority over strict real-world automotive physics.
* **Application**:
  * Animals react emotionally: grinning, roaring with blue eyes during nitro, and getting dizzy when spun out.
  * Raycast suspension produces springy, bouncy cartoon chassis dynamics that lean dramatically into turns without flipping or simulation stalling.
