# Animal Combat Racing — AI Driver Specification

## 1. Requirements
* Seamlessly steer along track splines.
* Avoid track barriers and nearby karts via raycast sensors.
* Support dynamic rubber-banding to keep racing pack tight.
* Use weapons opportunistically when targets are in range.

## 2. Phased Complexity
* **MVP**: Simple waypoint/spline following with steering angle calculation.
* **Phase 8+**: Collision avoidance and obstacle swerving.
* **Phase 11+**: Combat targeting and tactical weapon triggers.
